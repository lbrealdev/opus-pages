Formula. Monthly payment M = P × r / (1 − (1 + r)−n), where P is the financed balance, r is the annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100, and n is the term in months. With a 0% rate, M = P / n. The monthly fee is added on top of M. The effective annual cost is the rate i that makes the installments equal to the cash you actually receive, annualized as (1 + i)12 − 1. Results are estimates; lenders may round, compound, or charge fees differently.
