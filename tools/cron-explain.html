      English → Cron

      Best effort for common phrases: intervals, times, weekdays, days of the month, and months.

      Suggestion

      -

        Follows standard 5-field cron (as used by common Unix cron daemons). When both day of month and day of week are restricted, the job runs on days matching either field. Non-standard extensions such as seconds, years, L, W, and # are not supported. Next runs are computed in your browser’s time zone.
