ThroughputEstimated time

Assumptions. Time = size in bits ÷ (Mbps × 1,000,000). Size in bits = size × unit multiplier × 8, where the multiplier is 1000n (decimal) or 1024n (binary). Throughput is treated as a constant effective rate: protocol overhead, slow start, congestion, retransmits, disk speed, and per-file overhead for many small files are not modeled. The optional RTT note adds a rough connection-setup cost and shows the bandwidth-delay product; it does not simulate TCP.
